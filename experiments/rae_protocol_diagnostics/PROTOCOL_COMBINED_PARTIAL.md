# Partial two-factor information-set diagnostic

This post-score historical diagnostic compares four B3 input scenarios on the same 99 Chinese target--origin keys per horizon: (1) archived strict masks and vintages; (2) the S11 partial release-timing relaxation covering 104 source-verified later values; (3) the partial captured-vintage variant replacing 14 observed Brent cells; and (4) both changes applied together. These two maps affect disjoint input cells. The interventions are limited to recovered cells and the captured Brent vintage registry; they do not define the complete P0--P3 protocols.

Generate the combined scenario with the same 157-context batching order, target histories, official frozen TimesFM-3 checkpoint and source commit, decoding settings, and q50 strict-replay tolerance of 1e-6 percentage points. Verify every source and map hash, the disjoint cell keys, expected mask states, and strict replay parity. Generate and seal forecasts without opening scoring targets or accessing P1.

Score all four scenarios from the archived B3 signed forecast-minus-target residual and the matched forecast differences; do not export target values. Report MAE, RMSE, B3's rank among thirteen methods with the other twelve forecasts fixed, and paired absolute-loss contrasts for mask versus strict, vintage versus strict, combined versus strict, combined versus mask, combined versus vintage, and the two-factor interaction (combined loss minus mask-only loss minus vintage-only loss plus strict loss). The latter is an arithmetic interaction of the two partial interventions, not a causal estimate of general vintage or release effects. Compute pointwise 95% descriptive circular-block intervals for all contrasts and horizons using 30,000 draws, seed 417, 3-, 6-, and 12-month blocks, and the complete 105-month target calendar with missing positions retained. Forecasts, score inputs, code, outputs, and the protocol are hashed. The intervals are post-score and unadjusted for multiple comparisons.
