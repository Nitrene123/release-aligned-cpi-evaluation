# Release-aligned information-set diagnostic: frozen analysis plan

This post-score historical diagnostic uses the archived China 2018--2026 B3 strict-mask and verified later-value forecasts. The later-value variant is a deliberately unavailable-information diagnostic, not a real-time policy and not a complete latest-vintage scenario. The prior S11 results were available when this analysis was specified.

The sampling unit is the paired target--origin key at each horizon (1, 3, 6, 12). The primary comparison is the 99-key intersection shared by the thirteen methods and both B3 input variants. Match origin ID, origin month, target month, horizon, and, where supplied, origin timestamp. Check strict B3 losses against the existing score archive to 1e-7 percentage points before analysis. Record all exclusions. Baseline model losses remain fixed; only B3 changes between the two displayed scenarios, so rank shifts are conditional on the other methods retaining their original inputs. No retraining, tuning, new predictions, or P1 access is involved.

For each method and horizon report matched-sample MAE and RMSE. For five-seed models, average seed-specific absolute and squared errors within origin before taking the across-origin mean and square root, respectively. For B3 and other single-prediction models, squared loss is the square of absolute loss. Report B3's sample rank under both scenarios, retaining ties; report the number and sources of recovered cells from the frozen S11 recovery map.

For paired B3 loss changes, report mean and median, direction counts, and pointwise 95% descriptive circular moving-block bootstrap intervals for block lengths 3, 6, and 12. Use 30,000 draws per horizon/block length and seed 417. Construct blocks on the complete monthly target calendar and retain missing positions; compute each resampled mean from present paired losses. These intervals describe this historical diagnostic and are not preregistered confirmation or adjusted for multiple comparisons.

Full P0 latest-value, P1 release-lag-only, P2 vintage-aware, and P3 release-aligned evaluation require source-by-source version/availability coverage and a separately frozen input-construction contract. The S11 partial later-value scenario must not be relabeled as full P0. Eligibility coverage and common-intersection comparisons are reported separately when the full inputs become available.
